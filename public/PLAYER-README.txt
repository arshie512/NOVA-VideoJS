NOVA — THE LIVING FRAME

This download includes the interactive NOVA player, its visible play/pause,
volume, mute and fullscreen controls, and the complete video with soundtrack.

To open it on Windows:
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Double-click start-player.bat. Node.js must be installed.
3. Open http://localhost:4178/ in your browser.
4. Keep the launcher window open while playing. Close it with Ctrl+C.

Opening index.html directly can show a blank page because browsers restrict
the local scripts used by the interactive player. The MP4 by itself contains
the video and sound; the controls are in this player package.
